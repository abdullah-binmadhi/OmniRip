OmniRip Browser Audio Bridge

Route YouTube, SoundCloud, Twitch, and web videos directly through OmniRip's 64-bit EQ Engine to your iBasso DAC or MacBook Headphone Jack with zero latency.

DRAG THIS BUTTON TO YOUR BOOKMARKS BAR:

OmniRip EQ
  1. Show your Bookmarks Bar: Press Cmd + Shift + B in Zen Browser, Safari, or Chrome.
  2. Drag the button above: Drag the blue OmniRip EQ button into your bookmarks bar.
  3. Play any YouTube video: Go to YouTube or any video site and start playing.
  4. Click the bookmark: Click OmniRip EQ in your bookmarks bar. The audio will immediately stream through OmniRip with your active EQ and tube warmth!
